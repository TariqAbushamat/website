FAQ

Common Questions

Can I customize the names, verses, and flower colors on ring trays? +

Yes, absolutely! Every engagement ring tray is 100% custom-tailored. You can choose couple names, event date, custom Quranic verses or quotes in Arabic and English calligraphy, velvet ring box colors (mauve, dusty blue, beige, white), floral arrangements, and ribbon styles.

How far in advance should I place my order? +

We recommend at least 2 to 4 weeks before your event date. Rush orders are available—send us a DM on Instagram to check availability!

Where do you deliver or offer pickup? +

We serve all of Niagara Region and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA) including Toronto, Mississauga, Oakville, Burlington, and surrounding cities.
